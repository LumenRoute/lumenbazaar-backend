# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must keep participation respectful, technically honest, and welcoming.
Constructive disagreement is expected; harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Project expectations

- Represent test, testnet, API, and settlement evidence accurately.
- Do not submit copied, unreviewed, malicious, or knowingly insecure work.
- Respect contributor ownership, licensing, review decisions, and issue scope.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and provide reproducible evidence where possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that
violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct or
security-sensitive concerns through the private contact process in `SECURITY.md`.
